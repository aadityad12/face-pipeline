## Detector input size (issue #10)

The detector's ONNX input is `[1, 3, ?, ?]` (found in #1), so 640x640 is my choice, not a
requirement. Detection is 63% of a single-face frame, so this is the obvious speed knob.

Time to detect one face, and how many of 6 faces are still found as those faces get
smaller relative to the frame:

```
det size   detect ms    100% of frame   50%   33%   25%
640            123.2                6     6     6     6
480             71.6                6     6     6     6
320             41.0                6     6     5     1
256             28.2                6     6     0     0
```

480 is 1.7x faster than 640 and loses nothing on this data. 320 is 3x faster and starts
silently dropping distant faces.

**Keeping 640 anyway.** The failure mode of a smaller input is not an error or a warning,
it is a face that is simply never reported, and "person standing further away" is exactly
the case a door camera meets. The 1.7x is not worth a failure I cannot see. The size is a
constructor argument, so it is one line to change if the camera geometry is known, which
is the situation a board deployment would actually be in.
